# Awards: a supernova, news in the hero, and a page for the hackathon wins

- **Date:** 15 October 2026
- **Branch:** `feat/awards` (from `feat/landing-polish`)
- **Why:** Vitamin Bob won the World Bank Group's Small AI for Development Health track
  and then Hack-Nation's 7th Global AI Hackathon outright, and Meet is speaking at the
  World Bank's summit in Seoul on 21 October. Meet asked for an "awards" tab with a
  supernova as its body, and for signs of the news in the hero that feel natural. After
  three rounds of feedback the wins are one award with one page. That page is told the way
  the team tells it at the summit, put Meet's own part first, and is written in short
  paragraphs and bullets. The trip is shown on a globe, with no Gujarat on it. It's the
  first time photos of Meet appear on the site.

## What changed

- **A new kind of work, `awards`**, drawn as a **supernova** (`bodies.ts`): the star still
  burning at the centre with its spikes, ejecta streaming out in thin, uneven fingers, a
  ragged shell brightest at its rim with warm knots, and a faint outer shock. Its portrait
  (`portraits.ts`) adds a white-hot glow under it and two spikes of light over it. The
  kind is green (hue 135), with `*` as its mark. It's the last tab in the bar, **awards**.
- **One award, "Hack-Nation & World Bank Group Hackathon"** (`hackathon-wins` in
  `work.ts`), first in "Now". Its chapter on the home page lists both wins under its name.
  Its main link reads "The story", and its picture is the team photo, from the original
  HEIC (`public/work/vitamin-bob/team.webp`). In the bar, its preview is the two LinkedIn
  announcement cards side by side (`public/work/hackathon-wins/both.webp`). This comes from
  a unit's new `peek`, which overrides the picture its media would give (`peekOfUnit`).
- **The news** (`NEWS` in `work.ts`, read through `news.ts`) shows in three places until
  15 December:
  - **A line under the hero's lede**, `* New`, in the award's colour, which glides to the
    award's chapter. It comes in 450 ms after the hero settles. The hairline draws in from
    the left, "New" flares once (as a supernova does), the words are wiped in from the
    left, and the arrow drops into place. When the page moves on from the hero (the bar
    docks), it leaves the same way, faster. It comes back in on the way up. Under reduced
    motion it only fades.
  - **A new light in the sky behind Earth**: a small supernova that flares gently and is
    labelled "new". Pointed at, it is framed and named. Clicked, it flies to the award. It
    leaves as the camera pulls back, like the stars around it.
  - **A small light on the awards tab.**

  The server renders the line meant for before the talk. From 21 October the visitor's
  clock switches it to the line for after the talk, and the news goes away on its `until`
  date.

- **The award's page** (`/work/hackathon-wins`, `award.tsx`, `award.module.css`, used by
  `page.tsx` for any unit with a `story`). It is written to read like a short article. A
  rail beside the column (`steps.tsx`) names the parts and marks the one being read. On a
  narrow screen the rail becomes a row under the bar.
  - **Head:** "Two wins for Vitamin Bob", one paragraph, and links to Vitamin Bob and its
    code. The supernova turns beside it (`BodyPortrait high`).
  - **The wins:** two equal cards side by side, Hack-Nation and the World Bank Group. Each
    shows its own announcement image, then who gave it, what it is, and one line. Under
    that come links to the post on each platform where it was announced (`platforms.tsx`):
    LinkedIn and X for Hack-Nation, LinkedIn for the World Bank, which didn't post on X.
    The logos are from svglogos.dev, drawn inline. At rest they sit in the text's grey.
    Pointed at, a logo pops and tilts as it takes its own colour, a ring ripples out from
    it once, the arrow lifts, and a line draws in under the name.
  - **How it works:** one line on the problem, then an **animation of a call**
    (`call-flow.tsx`, `call-flow.module.css`), after the team's tech demo video, redrawn in
    the site's style: hairlines on black, Plex, the award's green for what is live and a
    muted red for an emergency. In eight steps, a line of caption each: Kevin gives a
    missed call (₹0); the district computer finds a free line; it calls him back in Hindi;
    he answers on his keypad; a dial decides how urgent it is; an emergency alerts the
    nearest clinic, or a routine case books an appointment; free, in his language, with no
    internet. It keeps cycling from step to step while it's on screen. A dot jumps to its step and it carries on from there, and it can be paused. It sits on a nearly solid panel, so the sky doesn't show through. On a phone the same
    picture is laid out top to bottom. Three bullets follow it.
  - **My part:** Meet's role (AI & Backend, LLM & Product Engineer), three bullets on what
    Meet built, the skills, and the team.
  - **The journey:** a countdown to the talk, and the globe. It is a glyph Earth with real
    coastlines (`public/earth-land.png`, Natural Earth 1:110m land, 3 KB) that turns to
    each day's place as it is read (`journey.tsx`). The flight PIT → ATL → ICN is drawn by
    the real date: dotted before it leaves, flown as it flies. Nothing turns on its own:
    only reading on, picking a day, or a drag. On a phone, the globe stays at the top and
    the days pass behind it. Then the team photo, and a note held for the Seoul pictures
    until 23 October.
- The impact chapter's line now says "or an award". `discovery.ts` names the kind "Award".
